FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Drop us a message above and we'll reply within 6 hours.

Do you offer wholesale or corporate coffee supply? +

Yes — we supply offices, hotels, and restaurants across Singapore. Visit our Supplier Portal to register your interest. Minimum order is 2kg/week for wholesale accounts. We provide equipment support and barista training.

How does the bean subscription work? +

Choose your preferred origin, grind (or whole bean), and frequency (fortnightly or monthly). Beans are roasted to order and dispatched within 48 hours. You can pause, skip, or cancel any time with no fee. First bag ships at 20% off.

Can I book a private event or cupping session? +

Absolutely. We host private cuppings, team coffee workshops, and branded pop-ups. Our Tiong Bahru and Dempsey locations have private event spaces. Email events@noirbrew.com with your date and group size.

Do you have vegan or dairy-free options? +

Yes — all three cafés offer oat, almond, and soy milk at no extra charge. Most drinks on our menu are vegan-adaptable. Look for the VG and DF tags on the menu page. We're also nut-allergy aware — ask your barista.

How fresh are the beans when I receive them? +

Every bag is roasted to order and dispatched within 48 hours of roasting. We recommend waiting 5–7 days post-roast for filter and 10–14 days for espresso before brewing — the CO₂ degasses and flavour blooms. Your bag includes the roast date.

Do you offer barista training or courses? +

Yes — we run SCA-certified Barista Skills and Brewing courses at our Dempsey location. Weekend courses are also available. Email hello@noirbrew.com with "Training" in the subject and we'll send you the schedule.

Is there WiFi at your cafés? +

All three locations have free, high-speed WiFi. Tiong Bahru and Dempsey have dedicated work areas with power points. Changi is naturally great for one-cup stopovers. We call it "Brew & Work" and we genuinely encourage it.

How can I apply to work at Noir Brew Co.? +

We love proactive applications. Send your CV and a short note on your coffee journey (and why Noir Brew) to hello@noirbrew.com with the subject "Careers". No formal experience required for front-of-house — passion and curiosity matter most.
